# Open E-Sign ERPNext
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
ERPNext users sending existing PDFs for signature, and external recipients signing without an ERP account.

## Product Purpose
An openly maintained Frappe signing app integrated with existing ERP records and documents.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve the upstream signing workflow, templates, permissions, and ERP triggers. Publish source and reproducible builds under AGPL-3.0. Local review comes before any deployment. Never claim DocuSign parity or legal certification.

## Brand Commitments
User-selected name: Open E-Sign ERPNext. User requests a less generic, less AI-looking interface. Direction assumption: a restrained, document-first workspace.

## Evidence on Hand
Upstream backend and compiled UI. New screenshots must use labelled fictional demonstration records, never customer data.
